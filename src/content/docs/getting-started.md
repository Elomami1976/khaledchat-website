> Written for Khaled Chat 0.1.1 on Windows.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Version 0.1.1 was tested on Windows 10; Windows 11 has not been tested yet.
- At least 8 GB of memory (RAM).
- Free disk space for the model you choose: about 2 GB for the smallest one.
- An internet connection for the downloads. After that, chatting works offline.

## Downloading and installing

1. On the [Download page](/download/), download the Windows installer (`KhaledChat-Setup-0.1.1-x64.exe`). You can compare its SHA-256 checksum with the one shown on the page.
2. Run it. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC". Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the installer. It installs for your user account only, so it doesn't need administrator rights. You can choose the folder, and it adds Start menu and desktop shortcuts.

## First launch

Khaled Chat checks your computer first: memory, processor, graphics card and free disk space. This takes a moment on older computers. Nothing is sent anywhere.

It then recommends one AI model that fits your computer, with its download size. Choose **Change model** to pick another. Models too big for your computer are listed but can't be selected.

## Downloading your first model

Choose **Download and start…**. A confirmation shows:

- the model's name and size;
- where it comes from;
- the folder it will be saved to;
- its license.

Nothing is downloaded until you choose **Download**. The button becomes active after a short moment, so it can't be clicked by accident.

The download shows its progress and can be cancelled. Every file is checked against its published SHA-256 checksum. The model then loads, and the status in the corner shows **Ready**.

## Your first conversation

Type a message and press Enter. The answer is written on your computer, word by word. **Stop** ends it early. Your chats are saved on your computer and listed in the sidebar.

## Optional: your name

In **Settings → General**, you can add your first name. The welcome screen then greets you by name, depending on the time of day. It is stored only on your computer and can be removed at any time.
