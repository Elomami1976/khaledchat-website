## Local storage

Khaled Chat stores its data on your computer rather than on a remote server.

## Conversation history

Conversations are saved locally so you can return to them. Deleting the application data removes them.

## User preferences

Settings, including your optional first name, are stored on your device.

## File handling

Files you share with Khaled Chat are processed locally and are not uploaded for AI processing.

## Optional network connections

Downloading models and application updates requires a connection to the servers hosting those files. Any future optional online features will be documented here.
