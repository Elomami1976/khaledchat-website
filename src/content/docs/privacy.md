> Written for Khaled Chat 0.1.0.

## Everything stays on your computer

These are stored on your computer only:

- your chats;
- files you add;
- the models;
- your settings;
- your optional first name.

Khaled Chat has no account, no cloud service, and no analytics or telemetry.

Data is kept in these folders:

- `%APPDATA%\Khaled Chat`: settings, chats and models;
- `Documents\Khaled Chat`: the files used in chats.

## Network use

Khaled Chat only uses the internet for:

- downloads you confirm;
- Web access, if you turn it on (off by default);
- connectors you add;
- links you open.

[Working Offline](/docs/working-offline/) lists every case. There are no automatic updates and no background downloads.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Files & storage → Delete all chats** removes every chat from Khaled Chat. Files you created stay in their folders.
- **Settings → Files & storage → Remove** deletes Khaled Chat's data and models, and the app itself. You are asked to confirm twice.
- Uninstalling from Windows keeps your chats and models, in case you reinstall. Use **Remove** first if you want them gone.

## How the app protects you

- Every download needs your confirmation.
- The app's window runs in a sandbox.
- Connecting an app that runs a program asks you first, in a Windows dialog.

The source code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/Elomami1976/khaledchat-desktop).
