> Khaled Chat is in development and has not been released. This section describes the intended behavior and will be verified against the first release.

## Initial setup requirements

An internet connection is required to download Khaled Chat and at least one AI model. Plan for the model download in advance, as model files can be several gigabytes.

## Offline usage

Once a compatible model is installed, supported local AI features are designed to run without an internet connection. Conversations are generated by the model on your computer.

## Local file handling

Documents and images you work with are read from your computer and processed locally.

## Network-independent functionality

Chatting with an installed model, browsing your conversation history and changing local settings do not depend on a network connection. Downloading new models and new versions of the application does.
